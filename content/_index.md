{{< biography lang="en" >}}
Welcome to my personal website. I see myself as a learner and someone who enjoys sharing what I learn. I also hope to keep becoming a better maker through practice. This blog is a place to collect my thoughts, make sense of what I learn, and share the small things I build. My writing lives in four sections: [Essays]({{< relref "/essays" >}}), for everyday reflections and passing thoughts that do not always need a conclusion; [Journal]({{< relref "/journal" >}}), for everyday updates and small steps forward; [Technical Notes]({{< relref "/tech" >}}), for ideas, code, and the process of building things; and [Just for Fun]({{< relref "/fun" >}}), for interactive projects you can try. I hope sharing these experiences can save someone a little trial and error. Whether it is a piece of code, a small project, or a few lines of writing, helping a reader in some small way makes it worth putting into words.
{{< /biography >}}

{{< biography lang="zh-CN" >}}
这里是 Bob Lee 的个人主页。我把自己看作一个学习者、分享者，也希望成为一个不断动手实践的创作者。这个博客是我留给自己的一块地方，用来记录想法，整理学到的东西，也分享做出来的小作品。我想把这里的文字分成四部分：[随笔]({{< relref "/essays" >}})留给日常的思考和零散的感受，不必每一篇都有一个明确的结论；[日志]({{< relref "/journal" >}})记录日常、近况与小小的进展；[技术笔记]({{< relref "/tech" >}})记录具体的实现过程与代码；[小乐趣]({{< relref "/fun" >}})收集可以亲手尝试的互动作品。我希望通过分享，让更多人少一些重复摸索，多一些可以参考的经验。无论是一段代码、一个小作品，还是一篇短短的文字，如果能给读到它的人带来一点帮助，就有了写下来的意义。
{{< /biography >}}
